## Responsibility

Retain, reconstruct and transfer complete personal work at explicit anonymous-browser local, native Android local and authenticated-service commit boundaries, including protected service pending work and context-appropriate recoverable history. Native complete selected song/set exchange uses independent destination copies with atomic local activation and qualified attachment completeness.

## Language

Library, backup, portable song/set, schema version, content revision, pending work, acknowledgement, expected revision, retry receipt, identity mapping, claim, conflict, recoverable deletion, restore and permanent purge.

## Boundaries

No silent conflict merge, cross-account recovery, imported authority, native live synchronization or instant offline revocation promise. Native local authority requires no service login and never unlocks retained service content. Persistence/session status is not a musical lifecycle.

## External Relationships

Library supplies identity, membership, capability and retention rules; Sketch supplies complete musical invariants. Private-service recovery remains service-authoritative; eligible private device work remains explicitly pending. Native local durability is authoritative on its installation but is not remote backup or service acknowledgement. Required PDF bytes/integrity participate in complete native transfer before acknowledgement. Operator backups retain a separate documented restore/retention contract.
