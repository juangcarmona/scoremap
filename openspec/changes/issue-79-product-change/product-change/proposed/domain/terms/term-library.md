## Definition

A personal collection of songs, repertoires, memberships and associated musical data, plus independently owned reusable resources and preferences. Anonymous browser authority is on the retained device origin; authenticated acknowledged authority is the private service. Standalone native Android authority is the installation's durable local library without account or backend. Active song presence excludes recoverably deleted songs but does not imply their erasure.

## Distinguish From

A device cache, pending draft and exported backup are not the current acknowledged private-service library. Native local acknowledgement is not service save, remote backup or synchronization. Shared service song access grants no access to other owner resources. Native independent-copy import creates destination-owned local content without account/grant/hardware authority.

## Usage

Empty active libraries and repertoires without songs are valid. Native sets preserve explicit membership order. Native songs/sets retain acknowledged local history until applicable confirmed purge; history restoration is a new revision, not erasure of intervening revisions. Personal preferences remain local in native transfer and principal-owned in service context rather than belonging to another musician's song.
