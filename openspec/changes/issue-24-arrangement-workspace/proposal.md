## Why
The integrated exact-pitch editor currently selects one part. Musicians need real arrangement configuration and synchronized consultation across independently assigned keyboards without changing musical meaning.

## What Changes
Add guarded arrangement/part/device/binding configuration, shared measure controls, explicit removal reconciliation and independent form duplication. Show all relevant keyboard assignments under one section/repeat/measure/event selection, with independent view ranges, exact held notes, explicit silence and retained off-screen notes.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `arrangement-workspace`: Independent parts/devices and synchronized split/layer consultation.

## Impact
Existing musical domain/application commands, feature-owned configuration and consultation UI, versioned backup/copy paths, synthetic unit/browser and real-service journeys. No audio engine, MIDI, fretboards or production deployment.
