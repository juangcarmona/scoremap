## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Independent arbitrary part and device collections
The workspace MUST configure independent parts, physical keyboards and valid sound/range/layer bindings without a maximum of three. Every mutation MUST preserve unaffected music and require current edit/capability admission. Referenced removals MUST disclose and confirm exact current reconciliation.

#### Scenario: Fourth assigned part
- **GIVEN** three independently performed/assigned parts, a valid unassigned fourth keyboard and independent arrangement Q
- **WHEN** a valid fourth part and binding are committed
- **THEN** all four retain independent identities and every prior note/configuration plus Q remains unchanged.

### Requirement: Shared read-only musical context
Every relevant keyboard MUST follow one identified section/repeat/measure/event selection while retaining its own range and assigned parts. Silence MUST clear obsolete highlights; held and out-of-view notes MUST remain identifiable without transposition or mutation.

#### Scenario: Silent third part
- **GIVEN** three assigned keyboards in the second occurrence of a repeated section
- **WHEN** the musician selects an event where only the first two parts perform
- **THEN** all views show the same context, the first two show their exact notes and the third identifies silence with no old performed highlights or content revision.

### Requirement: Complete identity-preserving copies and round trips
Arrangement copies MUST retain shared song references with new owned identities; independent song copies MUST consistently remap their complete owned graph. Supported persistence and backups MUST retain every configuration and performed occurrence without merging equal-pitch layers.

#### Scenario: Split and overlapping layers
- **GIVEN** independently owned notes and valid physical ranges
- **WHEN** split and overlapping layer assignments are saved and reopened
- **THEN** all original note identities/ownership/pitches remain and routing metadata is complete without fabricated duplicate notes.
