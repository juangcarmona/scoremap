# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Musicians prepare songs and consult their music while rehearsing or performing. ScoreMap organizes songs, sections, chords and arrangements so musicians can find and understand the music they need.

## Operating context

The application is used on desktop, landscape touch tablets and narrow phones. Performance consultation must remain readable at playing distance. A song may contain many chords and several keyboard parts.

## Capabilities and constraints

The accepted product model under `docs/product/model/` is authoritative. Consultation is protected from editing; private libraries require real identity and authorization. Clearly distinguish theoretical chord tones from the exact pitches played by each instrument. Preserve the musician’s content and acknowledge storage failures. PDF support and actual operator deployment remain separate work.

## Accessibility

Follow `QR-READABILITY`: high contrast, 48 CSS-pixel touch targets, WCAG 2.2 AA, visible keyboard focus, 200% text enlargement and accurate keyboard geometry.

## Evidence

Use synthetic songs and arrangements for public screenshots and design fixtures. Do not put personal music, identities or credentials in the repository.
