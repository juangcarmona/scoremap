## Definition

An authenticated identity distinguished by stable provider subject and tenant/issuer scope. It owns personal preferences/resources and may hold explicit song capabilities. Display name or email equality does not establish principal equality.

## Distinguish From

A client-submitted owner ID, cached role, global role, ingress identity and previous session are not current resource authorization.

## Usage

Protected pending work belongs to one principal and resource scope; only that reauthenticated principal with current server permission may recover it. Account switch never imports or displays the previous principal's drafts.
