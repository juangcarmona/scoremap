## Definition

An identified choice of exact spelled performed pitches, each with consistent octave and absolute pitch, independent of harmonic pitch-class identity. Register positions or constrains those pitches. Optional hand assignment is contextual metadata. A named reusable voicing is a principal-owned versioned preset; applying it copies its content/version provenance into the arrangement. Later preset edits/deletion never silently change arrangements.

## Distinguish From

A theoretical chord lists degrees/pitch classes without mandatory octaves. A selected piano range affects view, not the performed music.

## Usage

Cmaj7 may be performed C2/G2/E4/B4 across independent parts/devices while preserving C/E/G/B harmony. Octave/inversion edits require valid pitch bounds. Non-chord tones require explicit reconciliation; unknown manual-note identity is permitted as custom voicing, not an automatic harmonic rewrite.
