## Requirement

Use high contrast, restrained nearly square components, large touch targets of at least 48 CSS pixels, clear structure and rapid song switching at tablet landscape and phone widths.

## Measurement

Retain deterministic behaviour verification, browser interaction evidence and independent assessment; rendering or test counts alone do not establish acceptance.
