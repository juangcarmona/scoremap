## Constraint

Never fabricate real song metadata, arrangements or progressions. Examples and tests must use clearly synthetic fixtures. No proprietary user content is deployed with the application.

## Rationale

Approved product boundaries supplied by the owner on November 15, 2026.

## Consequences

Delivery specifications and architecture must preserve this boundary.
