## Constraint

The evolution includes Anglo-American/fixed-name Latin/dual notation, bounded extended harmony and manual-note candidate identification, exact voicings and reusable presets, integrated direct piano editing, song-owned form, independent arrangements/parts, multiple keyboards/splits/layers, private accounts and explicit capabilities, acknowledged cross-device service recovery, revisions, portability and protected private-service offline scope. The usable connected local checkpoint is a first vertical slice, not completion or removal of the rest. The original November 20, 2026 core-delivery priority is historical context, not a promise that the whole evolution meets that deadline.

Native Android installation and the standalone no-login local library are also in scope. Native acceptance includes complete local create/edit/save/reopen, ordered sets, recoverable song/set history and deletion, explicit purge, complete current-song/ordered-set editable import/export and supported owned PDF/reference outcomes. Preserve selected editor, 88-key piano/exact-hand pitches, compact staff, focused song-sheet/lyrics/associations and prepared-phrase outcomes; title/notes-only CRUD does not complete native parity. Preserve spelling, actual register, independent parts and bindings, valid empty structures, protected performance consultation and all supported musical associations.

Excluded are guitar/bass/fretboard visualizers, MIDI/hardware input/output or detection, Yamaha-specific integration, microphone/audio device integration, audio synthesis/playback, automatic transcription/recording/AI arrangements, effects/patch switching, realtime collaborative coediting, public publishing/catalogue/social sharing services, public share links, full invitation UX and subscriptions/billing. Native file sharing through document/share mechanisms is permitted and does not introduce those services. Play-store distribution remains a separate product/release decision rather than a prerequisite for the selected installation journey. Sound metadata and explicit service resource grants are included; hardware routing and shared repertoire collaboration are not. PDF consultation/complete supported transfer and existing physical-device/origin acceptance remain independently verifiable obligations.

## Rationale

The authorized native outcome extends accepted musical meaning and adds local authority without claiming that a minimal vertical slice completes every musical capability.

## Consequences

Milestone ordering does not silently narrow product acceptance. Tooling, product acceptance, implementation, deployment and release remain separate obligations. Existing web/private-service behavior is not retired or replaced by this native journey.
