## Rule

A supported PDF is stored locally with its owning song. Navigation stays between page one and the final page, remembers the last viewed page and supports previous, next and horizontal swipe. Corrupt or unsupported PDFs produce a recoverable error without losing song data.

## Rationale

Protect the musician’s content and preserve trustworthy consultation.

## Examples

Concrete accepted and refused outcomes are authored in the related Structured Behaviours.

## Exceptions

None.
