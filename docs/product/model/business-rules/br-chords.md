## Rule

A root is A through G with at most one sharp or flat, and the initial quality is major or minor. Major pitch classes are root plus 0, 4 and 7 modulo 12; minor uses 0, 3 and 7. Enharmonic spelling is retained. The piano reflects chromatic pitch and octave, with no black key between E/F or B/C.

## Rationale

Protect the musician’s content and preserve trustworthy consultation.

Concrete accepted and refused outcomes are authored in the related Structured Behaviours.

## Exceptions

None.
