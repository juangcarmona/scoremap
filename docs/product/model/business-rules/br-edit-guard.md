## Rule

Content mutations require edit mode. Performance mode rejects content creation, updates, deletion and import, including indirect mutation paths; navigation and remembered PDF page updates remain permitted. Switching mode never mutates musical content.

## Rationale

Protect the musician’s content and preserve trustworthy consultation.

## Examples

Concrete accepted and refused outcomes are authored in the related Structured Behaviours.

## Exceptions

None.
