## Constraint

Core delivery by Friday November 20, 2026 prioritizes repertoire, song, ordered sections, major/minor chords, accurate piano, notes, protected performance consultation, persistence and offline recovery. References and PDFs follow without compromising core delivery. Future only: USB MIDI, Yamaha CK88, live MIDI, chord detection/comparison, presets, effects, patch switching, native Android packaging, Google Play, sync, accounts, shared repertoires, collaboration, AI recognition, transcription, recording, subscriptions and billing.

Approved product boundaries supplied by the owner on November 15, 2026.

## Consequences

Delivery specifications and architecture must preserve this boundary.
