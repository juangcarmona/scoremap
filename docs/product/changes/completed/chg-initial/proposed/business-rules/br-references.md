## Rule

Only absolute HTTPS or HTTP references may be saved. External references open separately without losing pending local work. External content needs connectivity and is never claimed as available offline.

## Rationale

Protect the musician’s content and preserve trustworthy consultation.

## Examples

Concrete accepted and refused outcomes are authored in the related Structured Behaviours.

## Exceptions

None.
