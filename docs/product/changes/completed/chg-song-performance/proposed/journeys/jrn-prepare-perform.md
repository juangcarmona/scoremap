## Intended Outcome

A personally prepared song can be consulted quickly and safely on a tablet, with consistent notation, shared musical form and independent exact performed parts. Retained anonymous work remains editable and consultable without connectivity. Private content is available only within current resource-scoped authority; later qualified private offline consultation requires proven protected retention and the bounded authorization lease. The first connected private checkpoint does not enable persistent private offline access.

## Entry Conditions

A musician opens the product; an empty library is a supported beginning. Anonymous local work needs no private sign-in. Private reads require the authenticated stable principal's current resource permission. Musical mutations independently require applicable write capability, edit mode, complete validity and expected revision. A shared song grant never grants access to another principal's independent preferences or reusable resources.

## Journey Narrative

The musician organizes songs and non-owning repertoire memberships, prepares shared identified sections and musical events, and chooses spelled harmony separately from exact performed pitches. Independent arrangements retain their own parts, optional hand roles and device/range/layer bindings. Notation preferences change presentation without changing music. The musician reviews and deliberately commits valid work, sees its actual local or private-service acknowledgement, then consults synchronized keyboard views in protected performance mode and keeps an authorized portable backup.

For private work, real sign-in and current capabilities protect every direct and nested operation. A complete acknowledged service revision can be reopened in another authorized session. Pending device work is not described as remotely saved; stale or ambiguous outcomes require explicit revision/receipt reconciliation rather than silent overwrite or automatic replay. Private source claims are previewed, confirmed and non-destructive until authorized remote reconstruction is verified.

## Variants and Branches

Songs may have no repertoire, sections or chords. External references and PDFs remain optional with their independent connectivity and capability limits. One piano with left/right parts, two keyboards including one hand per instrument, three independent keyboards/sounds, splits, overlapping layers and parts without one-to-one hand mapping all serve the same journey. More than three parts/devices is valid; no unbounded viewport or capacity promise follows.

Anonymous continuation remains network-free on the retained origin. Qualified later private offline access covers only resources/permissions successfully checked online for that stable principal/session. It ends no later than eight hours from the authoritative server check and earlier on configured shorter bounds, known session/authorization expiry or observed logout/revocation/disablement. Local actions and restart cannot renew it; uncertainty and clock rollback lock. Reconnect requires fresh online checks before private exposure, export or replay. Unobserved offline revocation is not instantaneously detectable.

Expiry, unknown authority, logout and account switch lock private content and preserve only protected same-principal pending work without forced plaintext export, silent discard or blocked termination. Recovery requires the same reauthenticated provider subject and tenant/issuer, fresh resource/revision checks and explicit reconciliation. Original anonymous work is not erased. Recoverable private deletion retains owner-only history until separately confirmed purge; restoration retains identities but not former grants or memberships.

The first connected checkpoint uses the same selected users fixture for manual trials and real automated login and one validated application configuration. It demonstrates private create/edit, acknowledged save, reload/second session, logout and refused roles with synthetic data. It neither completes the remaining arrangement/offline scope nor requires production deployment or public-origin allocation. Development fixtures are not Production defaults.

## Completion Conditions

The intended complete content is acknowledged and recoverable at its declared boundary and readable in protected performance consultation while authorized. Anonymous device durability and private-service recovery remain distinguishable. Failed/refused commands preserve acknowledgement and protected recoverable work without a lifecycle transition. Expired private access remains locked, not considered successful consultation merely because a record exists. Full musical, protected-offline, accessibility and recovery acceptance requires its actual evidence; the connected checkpoint alone does not establish full completion, publication or deployment.

The musician can review a focused song sheet with known key, sections, repeats, chords, lyrics and cues; prepare both hands using the full-range piano and compact score; and compare prepared phrases across score and piano. These views preserve the same authorization, guard, acknowledgement and recovery boundaries above.
