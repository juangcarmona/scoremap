## Definition

An independently identified musical work reminder with required nonblank title, optional artist, annotations/references and a shared section/measure/harmonic-event timeline. Its arrangements reference that form rather than silently copying it. Private songs have a server-derived owner and explicit capabilities. Recoverably deleted content remains retained until separate owner purge.

## Distinguish From

An arrangement owns performed interpretation, not an independent mutable song form. A screen selection and a save status are not a song state.

## Usage

Reordering preserves identity. Explicit song duplication makes independent form with new identities and remapped associations. Deleting a repertoire does not delete a song.
