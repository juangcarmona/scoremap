## Definition

A personal collection of songs, repertoires, memberships and associated musical data, plus independently owned reusable resources and preferences. Anonymous authority is on the retained device origin; authenticated acknowledged authority is the private service. Active song presence excludes recoverably deleted songs but does not imply their erasure.

## Distinguish From

A device cache, pending draft and exported backup are not the current acknowledged private library. Shared song access does not grant access to the owner's other library resources.

## Usage

Empty active libraries and repertoires without songs are valid. Personal preferences remain principal-owned rather than belonging to another musician's song.
