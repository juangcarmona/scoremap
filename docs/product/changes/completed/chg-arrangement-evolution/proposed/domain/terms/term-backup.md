## Definition

A versioned complete portable export of its declared authorized musical scope, including every new musical field, owned preference and supported attachment with all required bytes/associations. It excludes credentials, sessions, reusable access material and authority-conferring grants.

## Distinguish From

A service volume is not a backup; an export is not proof of remote save. Operator disaster recovery backups and the musician's portable exports have distinct retention/restore responsibilities.

## Usage

Anonymous import is explicit confirmed complete replacement. Private claim/import previews a bounded authorized operation and never derives permissions from imported values. Unsupported content is refused before activation.
