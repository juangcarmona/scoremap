## Constraint

The evolution includes Anglo-American/fixed-name Latin/dual notation, bounded extended harmony and manual-note candidate identification, exact voicings and reusable presets, integrated direct piano editing, song-owned form, independent arrangements/parts, multiple keyboards/splits/layers, private accounts and explicit capabilities, acknowledged cross-device recovery, revisions, portability and protected offline scope. The usable connected local checkpoint is a first vertical slice, not completion or removal of the rest. The original November 20, 2026 core-delivery priority is historical context, not a promise that the whole evolution meets that deadline.

Excluded are guitar/bass/fretboard visualizers, MIDI/hardware input/output or detection, Yamaha-specific integration, audio synthesis/playback, automatic transcription/recording/AI arrangements, effects/patch switching, realtime collaborative coediting, public publishing/catalogue/social sharing, public share links, full invitation UX, subscriptions/billing and native Android/Play packaging. Sound metadata and explicit resource grants are included; hardware routing and shared repertoire collaboration are not. Optional PDF and existing physical-device/origin acceptance remain independently owned.

## Rationale

The full authorized evolution extends the alpha rather than pretending its earlier exclusions already allowed it.

## Consequences

Milestone ordering does not silently narrow product acceptance. Tooling, product acceptance, implementation, deployment and release remain separate obligations.
