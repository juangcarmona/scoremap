`CHG-ANDROID-NATIVE-LOCAL` was validated as an overlay with zero diagnostics,
reviewed, explicitly approved by the operator under the owner's existing
product/delivery delegation, dry-run and applied through
`@prodshape/integration-openspec` 0.6.6. The accepted model has 128 artifacts,
with zero errors or warnings. The change adds four native structured behaviours
and modifies 23 existing artifacts; 203 unchanged typed impacts are pinned and
explained in its manifest.

- [Exact decision](product-decision.json): identifies the pre-apply candidate,
  baseline, proposed digests, authority and decision sources. This is an operator
  decision, not a claim of personal owner inspection of unpublished bytes.
- [Actual apply receipt](product-apply.json): identifies the provider diff,
  dry-run, actual apply and full provider-receipt hash. Apply is not merge.
- [Hosted change](../../../openspec/changes/issue-79-product-change/proposal.md):
  records recovery from a failed native producer and the subsequent operator
  corrections. That producer remains failed; no SDK publication/approval
  binding or native epic acceptance receipt was fabricated.

The semantic review distinguished prior reviewer-authored repairs from its
independent examination of coordinator changes. The coordinator cross-reviewed
native song/persistence flows, retention, integrity, lifecycle and SB repairs.
Independent neighbour review found and corrected the missing native journey.
The final review reported no remaining P1/P2 in that bounded scope; it does not
attest Android implementation or device execution.

After apply, owning-provider citation emission re-grounded 303 existing bindings
in 92 current OpenSpec/ArcShape consumer files and added the four native SB
bindings to the foundation. Browser/private branches retain their declared
meaning; actual implementation descriptions remain distinct from the accepted
native target. `verify:product`, `verify:citations`, `verify:architecture` and
`verify:specs` passed locally. Required full CI and exact-head integration are
separate evidence recorded on the resulting PR.
